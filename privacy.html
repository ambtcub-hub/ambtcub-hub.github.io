Remy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Remy is a private tool that two people (the owners of one YouTube channel) use to manage their own channel. It is not offered to the public. Nobody other than the channel's owners signs in to it.

What Remy accesses through Google

When the channel owner signs in with Google, Remy can access, for that channel only:

  • Channel and video details and statistics (YouTube Data API).
  • Performance figures such as views and average view percentage (YouTube Analytics API).
  • Reach reports such as thumbnail impressions and click-through rate (YouTube Reporting API).
  • Comments on the channel's videos, and captions of the channel's own videos.

What Remy does with it

  • Writes reports for the channel owners on how their videos performed and why.
  • Sorts new comments and drafts answers to viewer questions, which the owners post themselves if they choose.
  • Suggests comments that look like spam or abuse. A comment is only removed after an owner approves it.
  • Suggests ideas for future videos.

Remy never uploads, edits or deletes videos, playlists, thumbnails, captions or channel settings, and never bans anyone.

Who else sees it

The data is used only to provide these features to the channel's owners. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else, with one exception needed to provide the features: comment text and channel figures are sent to an AI model provider (Anthropic, maker of Claude) so it can sort comments, draft answers and write the reports. That provider processes it only to return results to Remy.

Remy's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored on the owners' own private server. The Google sign-in token is kept in a file only the owners can read. Stored data is deleted when the owners stop using Remy or ask for it to be deleted.

You can remove Remy's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

YouTube

Remy uses YouTube API Services. By using Remy you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
